# Spera visual style (HSI family, dignified data)

Reference: the Hack for Social Impact brand mark (white ground, black grotesque
type, code-symbol confetti). Do NOT copy their logo; borrow the language.

## Ground rules

- Background: pure white (#FFFFFF), always. No dark screens.
- Type: bold grotesque sans-serif (Inter / Archivo style), black (#111111),
  very large headlines, tight tracking. Headline first, body minimal.
- Accent palette (use sparingly):
  - Hot pink #EC4899 — the primary accent (hearts, final bar, key numbers)
  - Mint teal #2DD4BF — secondary accent
  - Lime green #A3E635 — tertiary accent
  - Black #111111 — all text and chart bars
- Confetti symbols: [], {}, <>, =, ;, !, small outlined hearts — in the
  three accent colors plus black. Confined to hero/frame edges and corners.
  NEVER inside data panels, charts, or the report body.

## Per screen

- Dashboard: huge black headline, stat cards, gradient bar chart
  (black bars, final 21.1% bar in hot pink).
- Import wizard: centered dashed upload card, huge black title,
  3 step indicators, one small pink heart accent.
- Report page: corner accents only; chart and footnote stay clean black
  on white so the page survives printing and skeptical readers.

## Principle

Playful frame, serious data. The symbols say "we belong to this event";
the clean numbers say "you can trust this evidence".
